Skill Forge

Every power here was grown from a random seed. The forge picks a keystone effect, a shape that can carry it and a few extras, then spends a power budget on the numbers and names the result. None of it comes from a list of finished skills.

How was grown

    Kept powers

      Fusion keeps the first power's shape. The second power goes off on impact, when the first one ends, or when it defeats someone.

      Codes rebuild the exact same power on any device: SEED.TIER for a forged power, a trailing > for each evolution, and (A)x(B) for a fusion. Numbers assume fighters with 1000 HP.
